# M11 — Investment readiness

Status: **NOT STARTED**
Dependency: reliable production evidence and real-world product/business data

## Objective

Make architecture, reliability, security, costs, risks, roadmap, and outcomes
due-diligence ready without inventing metrics.

## Planned scope

- Reliability, source coverage, security, and architecture reports.
- Product metric definitions and customer-outcome tracking.
- Cost model and unit-economics templates.
- Investor data-room structure and product roadmap.
- Technical risk register and data-moat strategy.

## Exit criteria

- Claims trace to auditable technical or real-world evidence.
- Revenue, customer, usage, retention, conversion, testimonial, and outcome
  fields remain explicitly unknown until measured.
- Metrics requiring real users are clearly marked.
